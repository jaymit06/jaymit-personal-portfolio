01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

Part analytical.
Part imaginative.

I'm pursuing a B.Tech in Data Science at NMIMS, Mumbai, where I'm building my foundation in data analysis, machine learning, and turning real-world data into useful insights. I like the moment when a messy problem starts to make sense — asking better questions, spotting a pattern, and seeing what it reveals.

Outside coursework, I'm exploring music production and songwriting. I'm interested in the overlap between technology and creativity: how patterns become stories, how small experiments lead somewhere unexpected, and how both data and sound can reveal something you didn't notice at first.
